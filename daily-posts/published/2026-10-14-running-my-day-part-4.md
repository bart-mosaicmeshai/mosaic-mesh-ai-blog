# Running My Day, Part 4: What Happens to Today at the End of Today

![Two days from a BartBot Dailys list. Behind, 8 Sept Tues opened to its lines. In front, 9 Sept Wed opened to eight lines, five of them moved over from Tuesday, including Plan next team practice](/assets/bartbot-dailys/part-4/hero.png)
*Moving from Tuesday to Wednesday.*

*This is part four of a series about how I actually use the Lists tool in [BartBot](https://bartbot.mosaicmeshai.com). In each post I ask and answer one question.*

**The question:** What do I do with everything on today's list when today is over?

My answer is that every line on the day gets a decision, and then I delete the day itself. A line I finished is done, and I delete it. A line I didn't get to is deferred, and I move it to the day I'll do it. A line I've decided not to do at all is denied, and I delete that too. A line someone else can carry is delegated, and it stays on my list, because I'm still the one accountable for it. I only delete the day once nothing is left under it. That way I can be present and focused on the day I'm in.

## Four decisions

Three of the four, done, deferred and delegated, are adapted from David Allen's *Getting Things Done*. Denied is my name for the fourth: deciding not to do something at all.

Tuesday showed done and deferred. Done usually means deleted, with one exception.

**Done, but kept.** On March 6, 2027, ["Tax 2025"](https://bartbot.mosaicmeshai.com/lists/shared/fzXQgOpIjgRw8R8Ij300?item=8405) is struck through, next to "Tax 2026". I keep last year's while I do this year's, because I refer to it. When I finish 2026, I'll delete 2025, and 2026 will sit struck through next to 2027. A finished line stays only while the record still has a job.

![The 6 Mar Sat 2027 node expanded, with Tax 2025 struck through above Tax 2026](/assets/bartbot-dailys/part-4/tax-2025.png)
*Last year's taxes, kept beside this year's.*

Denied and delegated didn't come up on Tuesday, so these two examples are from other days. They aren't on the public list or in the video.

**Denied.** A call with a friend was on my list for noon. By 12:30 he hadn't called, so I deleted it. I didn't move it to another day, because I knew we'd catch up later in the week anyway. I also deny a line when I cancel something I'd planned, when it keeps slipping and I decide to stop moving it, when someone else decides it doesn't need to happen, or when my plans change.

**Delegated.** This one is rare, because most of what's on my list I have to do myself. When I can hand something off, the line stays on my list, because I'm still accountable for it. "Mow lawn" stays. What changes is the action: a text to my kids asking who can help today, and then helping them follow through.

## During the day, and at the end of it

Most of these decisions don't wait for the end of the day. That call got denied at 12:30. And many days I hit a point where I have to let the day go. That's when I start deferring things. It isn't unusual for me.

At the end of the day, or early the next morning, I decide everything that remains on the day, all at once. I go through every line, give each one of the four decisions, and then delete the day. I don't go top to bottom. I make the easy decisions first and leave the harder ones for last.

## What that buys me

Yesterday left at the top of my list is a very loud signal that I've left something undecided. That signal makes it very hard to be present and focused on what I'm doing now. It's like cooking dinner and leaving the kitchen a mess. Dinner isn't really done until the kitchen is clean and the dishes are washed. So the longest a day can sit there is until the next morning. I can't start the next day with yesterday still sitting there staring at me.

## My question for you

When your day ends, what happens to what's still on your list? Does it move, sit there, or quietly disappear? Drop your answer in a comment. I'd love to learn what you do with what's left.
